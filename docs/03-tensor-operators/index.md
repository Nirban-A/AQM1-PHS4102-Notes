# Part III: Tensor Operators, Wigner-Eckart and the Projection Theorem

This part covers how operators transform under rotations: scalar and vector operators, Cartesian tensors and their decomposition into irreducible pieces, irreducible spherical tensor operators and their commutation relations with $\hat{\vec J}$, the Wigner-Eckart theorem (with the dipole matrix element and the selection rules as the main application), and the projection theorem (with the spin expectation value and the Landé factor as applications). It continues directly from Part II on angular momentum and Clebsch-Gordan coefficients.
