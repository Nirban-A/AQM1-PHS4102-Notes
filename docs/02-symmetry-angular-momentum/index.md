# Part II: Symmetry, Angular Momentum and Clebsch-Gordan Coefficients

This part covers symmetry and conservation laws in quantum mechanics: translation and time translation (momentum and energy conservation, the time-evolution operator), rotations and the angular momentum algebra, spin-½ and the Pauli matrices, the $j=1$ representation, rotation matrices, orbital angular momentum and spherical harmonics, addition of angular momenta with Clebsch-Gordan coefficients, spin-orbit coupling (the sodium doublet, the Zeeman and Paschen-Back limits) and central potentials.
