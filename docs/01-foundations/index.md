# Part I: Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

The operator formalism of quantum mechanics: the postulates and Dirac notation, measurement and collapse, expectation values and uncertainty, compatible and incompatible operators (with the uncertainty relation), the Dirac delta function and the position and momentum representations, time evolution (the time-evolution operator, stationary states, the Schrödinger and Heisenberg pictures), the one-dimensional time-independent Schrödinger equation, the linear harmonic oscillator by the ladder-operator method, and coherent states.
